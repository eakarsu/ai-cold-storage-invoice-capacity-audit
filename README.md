# Cold Storage Invoice & Capacity Audit

Recalculate pallet-day storage, handling, temperature services, and capacity commitments across cold facilities.

**Primary buyer:** Food, pharmaceutical, and cold-chain operators. **Evidence:** storage agreements, pallets, lots, temperatures, receipts, shipments, daily inventories, blast freezing, handling, energy surcharges, invoices, and credits.

Full local application built with React, Vite, Express, PostgreSQL, and OpenRouter. Includes 15 domain-specific capabilities, 105 custom AI workbench fields, three scenario-fill controls per feature, operational registers, workflow transitions, analytics, professional AI decision briefs, audit history, and at least 15 PostgreSQL records per capability.

## Domain capabilities

- Storage agreement library
- Facility zone registry
- Lot pallet ingestion
- Daily inventory reconstruction
- Pallet-day calculation
- Minimum capacity validation
- Blast freezing charge
- Inbound handling audit
- Outbound handling audit
- Temperature excursion credit
- Energy surcharge validation
- Invoice recalculation
- Facility dispute workflow
- Credit reconciliation
- Facility product analytics

Run `./start.sh`, then open <http://127.0.0.1:4700>. API: `5700`.

Administrator: `runtime-admin@example.com` / `LocalDemo!2026`. Operator and reviewer credential buttons are available on the login page.
